Get to know the country

At the heart of Africa

The Democratic Republic of the Congo is a vast Central African nation shaped by the Congo River and the world's second-largest tropical rainforest. Its extraordinary landscapes support rich wildlife and diverse communities.

A land of remarkable scale

Where the river meets the rainforest

The Congo River flows through a vast network of waterways, forests, and fertile land. The country's varied habitats are home to rare species found nowhere else on Earth.
