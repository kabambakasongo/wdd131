Tech Atlas — Site Plan

Site Name

TA · Tech Atlas

The name suggests a map of technology: a friendly guide that helps people find their way through unfamiliar subjects. The "TA" monogram works as a compact logo in the site header.

Site Purpose

Tech Atlas is a small informational website that explains everyday technology to curious beginners. It offers short guides on the web, computing, artificial intelligence, security, and data, plus a form that recommends a first topic based on the visitor's goals.

Scenarios

  • How does the internet actually deliver a web page to my device?
  • What is the difference between artificial intelligence and ordinary software?
  • How can I protect my accounts from common online threats?
  • Which technology subject should I explore first?

Color Schema

  • Navy (#0b1f3a) — header, footer, headings, and dark cards.
  • Blue (#1f5fbf) — links, active filters, and primary buttons.
  • Cyan (#4cc9f0) — highlights and icon tiles on dark backgrounds.
  • Paper (#f4f7fb) — page background and form fields.
  • Ink (#16283f) — body text.

Typography

  • Montserrat — headings, navigation, and buttons.
  • Source Sans 3 — body text, descriptions, and form fields.
