Discover the DRC — Site Plan

Site Name

Discover the DRC — a name that invites visitors to explore the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Site Purpose

The site introduces the Democratic Republic of the Congo to potential travelers. It presents key destinations, shares practical travel guidance, and lets visitors record their travel interests.

Scenarios

  • What are the most interesting natural places to visit in the DRC?
  • Which destinations match my interest in wildlife or rivers?
  • How can I save the places and activities I care about before I travel?

Color Schema

  • Forest green (#123c35) — headers, buttons, and primary accents.
  • Deep green (#0a2b27) — header bar and footer background.
  • Leaf green (#26765b) — active navigation and secondary accents.
  • Lime (#d7ed9f) — tags and highlights on dark backgrounds.
  • Paper (#f5f4ec) — page background.
  • Ink (#20302b) — body text.

Typography

  • Montserrat — headings and navigation.
  • Source Sans 3 — body text and form fields.

Wireframes

Mobile view

Single column: header with menu button, hero image, stacked content cards, stacked form fields, footer.

Wider view

Two columns: header with inline navigation, wide hero, multi-column card grid, form beside a tips panel, footer.

Pages

  • Home (index.html) — introduction, key facts, and highlights.
  • Destinations (destinations.html) — filterable list of places built with JavaScript.
  • Plan Your Visit (plan.html) — form with validation and saved preferences.
